In plain words

Supplements raise vitamin D levels. For the many people who start low, this improves energy. People who already have enough vitamin D should not expect more energy.

A · Do supplements raise vitamin D?

NHANES, USA 2021–23 · % with low vitamin D (30–49 nmol/L)

Yes. Supplement users are about 3× less likely to be low.5.8% of supplement users vs 15.6% of the whole population (Couch et al., 2026).

B · How many people are low?

RKI DEGS1, German adults, n = 6,995 (%)

About 6 in 10 Germans are below the recommended level.3 in 10 are clearly deficient (< 30 nmol/L) (Rabenberg et al., 2015).

C · Does fixing low vitamin D lift energy?

PubMed RCT, tired adults with low vitamin D · % feeling less tired

Yes. 72 of 100 felt less tired vs 50 of 100 on placebo.Measured after 4 weeks; p = 0.01 (Nowak et al., 2016).

Who benefits?

The effect depends on your starting vitamin D level

Starting blood levelExpected energy benefitEvidence
Deficient (< 30 nmol/L)Yes — most likelyNowak et al. (2016); Roy et al. (2014); Wang et al. (2026)
Insufficient (30–49 nmol/L)PossibleWang et al. (2026)
Enough (≥ 50 nmol/L)No extra benefitD-Health trial, n = 21,315 (Neale et al., 2022)

Conclusion

GRADE-inspired rating (Guyatt et al., 2008)

Supplements raise serum vitamin D (Couch et al., 2026)High
Energy improves when a deficiency is corrected (Nowak et al., 2016; López-Muñoz et al., 2023)Moderate
No energy benefit expected in replete adults (Neale et al., 2022; Wang et al., 2026)Low / none
